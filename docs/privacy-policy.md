# FlySight Explorer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy describes how the FlySight Explorer iOS app (the "App") handles
your data. The App is published by 360world Europe Kft. ("360world", "we").
Contact: [info@360.world](mailto:info@360.world)

## In short

**We do not collect any data.** The App has no account, no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it does not send any
data to us or to anyone else. Everything the App stores stays on your device.

The App is open source, so you can check this yourself:
[github.com/360world/flysight-explorer-ios-app](https://github.com/360world/flysight-explorer-ios-app)

## Data stored on your device

The App keeps the following only in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad:

- **Jump recordings** that you download from your FlySight or import: GPS
  positions, altitude, speed and time of each recording.
- **Your FlySight devices**: an identifier assigned by iOS and the nicknames
  you give them.
- **Settings**, such as language and display options.
- **A diagnostic log** of downloads, saves and file reads, to help find
  problems.

We have no access to any of it. You can delete recordings in the App, and
uninstalling the App deletes all of the above. Like other app data, it may be
included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup), depending on
your iOS settings; Apple's privacy policy applies to those backups.

## Bluetooth

The App uses Bluetooth only to communicate with your FlySight: to list and
download its files, show live data and upload settings you change. Nothing
is sent anywhere else.

## Location

The App does not access your phone's location. The positions you see come
from recordings made by your FlySight.

## Maps

Maps are displayed with Apple Maps (MapKit). To draw the map, your device
loads map images for the area being shown directly from Apple. We do not
receive this information. Apple's privacy policy applies:
[apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Sharing

The App shares data only when you choose to — for example when you share a
recording's CSV file or share or copy the diagnostic log. Where it goes is up
to you. If you send us a file or log for support, we use it only to answer
your request and delete it when it is no longer needed.

## Children

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your data, there is nothing we can access,
correct or delete on our side; you control the data on your device. If you
have contacted us by email, you can ask us to access or delete that
correspondence at [info@360.world](mailto:info@360.world). You also have the
right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — in Hungary, the
Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH).

## Changes to this policy

If the App's handling of data changes, we will update this policy and its
effective date before releasing that version. Previous versions are available
in the
[repository history](https://github.com/360world/flysight-explorer-ios-app/commits/main/docs/privacy-policy.md).
