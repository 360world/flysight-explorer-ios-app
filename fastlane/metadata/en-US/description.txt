Your FlySight 2, without the card reader.

FlySight Explorer connects to your FlySight 2 GPS logger over Bluetooth. Download your jumps, analyse them, replay them on a big, readable HUD and change the device's settings — all from your phone, right at the dropzone.

DOWNLOAD OVER BLUETOOTH
• Browse the files on the FlySight's SD card and download a track with one tap
• Downloaded recordings are marked, so you never download the same one twice
• Works with several devices: give each one a nickname, and the app reconnects to the one you used last

ANALYSE EVERY JUMP
• Map of your flight path, coloured by phase: climb, freefall, deployment, canopy, landing
• Exit, deployment and landing are detected automatically
• Charts for speed, glide ratio, altitude and distance, with live values under the cursor
• Jump to freefall, opening, canopy or landing with one tap, or select any section yourself
• Statistics for the selected section: duration, altitude lost, speeds and glide ratio

REPLAY ON THE HUD
• Large numbers you can read at a glance
• Four layouts: Altitude, Glide, Edge bar and Dial
• Playback at 1–10× speed with a mini map
• Altitude above ground or above sea level

CONFIGURE YOUR FLYSIGHT
• Edit the complete CONFIG.TXT: GPS, tones, speech, thresholds, alarms and more
• Every setting explained, with its unit
• Alarm altitudes shown relative to your dropzone's elevation
• Load the configuration from the device, edit it and upload it back

YOUR LOGBOOK
• All downloaded jumps in one place, on your phone
• Import tracks from the Files app, iCloud Drive or other apps
• Rename, delete or share the raw CSV of any jump
• Built-in wingsuit and tracking samples to try the app without a device

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your jumps stay on your phone.

Available in English and Hungarian. Open source: github.com/360world/flysight-explorer-ios-app

Requires a FlySight 2 for Bluetooth features. CSV files from FlySight 1 and FlySight 2 can be imported.
